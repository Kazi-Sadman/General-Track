TOPIC 1: MINIMUM ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT
================================================================================

AIUB requires students to maintain a minimum attendance threshold of 80% in
their registered courses.

The 80% attendance requirement applies to both undergraduate and graduate
courses.

Students are expected to attend lectures and laboratory sessions regularly.

Attendance for laboratory sessions and regular lectures may be calculated
individually or together depending on the applicable department rules.

A student who does not meet the required attendance percentage may become
ineligible to sit for examinations.


KEYWORDS:
minimum attendance, attendance requirement, 80 percent attendance,
80% attendance, undergraduate attendance, graduate attendance,
lecture attendance, laboratory attendance, lab attendance,
exam eligibility


================================================================================
TOPIC 2: ATTENDANCE CALCULATION
================================================================================

Attendance percentage is calculated using the following formula:

Attendance Percentage (%) =
(Number of Classes Attended / Total Number of Classes Held) × 100

Example:
If a student attends 16 classes out of 20 total classes:

Attendance Percentage = (16 / 20) × 100 = 80%

Therefore, the student's attendance percentage is 80%.

TOPIC 3: ATTENDANCE TRACKING THROUGH VUES
================================================================================

Faculty members record student attendance digitally through the VUES
(Virtual University Expert System) portal.

Attendance is generally recorded during or immediately after each class
session.

Students can check their attendance percentage for each enrolled course
through their individual VUES student dashboard.

VUES therefore provides students with access to their course attendance
information and allows them to monitor their attendance status.


KEYWORDS:
VUES attendance, Virtual University Expert System, attendance portal,
attendance percentage, student dashboard, attendance monitoring


================================================================================
TOPIC 4: ATTENDANCE SHORTAGE AND CONSEQUENCES
================================================================================

Attendance status may be categorized according to the student's attendance
percentage.

80% to 100% Attendance:
- Considered satisfactory attendance according to the stated policy.
- Student remains eligible for examinations and continuous evaluation,
  subject to other applicable requirements.

70% to 79% Attendance:
- Considered a borderline attendance shortage.
- Student may receive warning notifications through the VUES portal.

Below 80% Attendance:
- If attendance is below 80% without approved official justification,
  the student may become ineligible to sit for the Final Examination
  in that course.

Students should monitor their attendance regularly through VUES to avoid
falling below the required attendance threshold.


KEYWORDS:
attendance shortage, attendance below 80, 70 percent attendance,
79 percent attendance, exam eligibility, final exam eligibility,
attendance warning, VUES warning


================================================================================
TOPIC 5: UNOFFICIAL WITHDRAWAL (UW) DUE TO ATTENDANCE SHORTAGE
================================================================================

If a student's attendance falls severely below the required minimum
threshold, typically below approximately 70%–75% before a Midterm or
Final Examination, and there is no prior official permission or approved
justification, the university may register a UW (Unofficial Withdrawal)
status.

A UW grade may prevent the student from taking the Midterm or Final
Examination for that course.

Implications of UW:

1. The student loses the right to take the applicable Midterm or Final
   Examination for the course.

2. The course counts as an academic attempt and must be retaken in a
   subsequent term according to the applicable academic rules.

3. Tuition fees already paid for the course are non-refundable.

4. A UW status may negatively affect eligibility for academic honors,
   Dean's List recognition, and graduation distinctions.


KEYWORDS:
UW, Unofficial Withdrawal, attendance UW, UW grade, attendance shortage,
course withdrawal, dropped course, retake UW, exam debarment


================================================================================
TOPIC 6: MEDICAL AND EMERGENCY ATTENDANCE EXEMPTION
================================================================================

Absences caused by severe illness, hospitalization, major surgery, or
bereavement in the immediate family may be reviewed for official approval.

Valid supporting documentation must be submitted promptly.

Examples of potentially acceptable emergency circumstances include:

- Severe illness
- Hospitalization
- Major surgery
- Bereavement in the immediate family


================================================================================
TOPIC 7: PROCEDURE FOR MEDICAL OR EMERGENCY ABSENCE APPROVAL
================================================================================

When a student is unable to attend classes because of a serious illness
or emergency, the following procedure applies according to the stated
policy:

Step 1:
The student or authorized guardian should notify the respective Department
Head and Course Instructor within 3 working days from the onset of the
illness or emergency.

Step 2:
The student should submit an official written application to the Office
of the Head of Department.

The application should include appropriate verified documentation, such as:

- Medical certificate
- Hospital admission papers
- Hospital discharge papers
- Other relevant official documents

Step 3:
The submitted documents are reviewed and verified.

If the Department Head and Dean approve the request, the relevant missed
classes may be treated as Excused Absence.

Where applicable, the student may also be permitted to complete make-up
evaluations or examinations.


KEYWORDS:
medical exemption, attendance exemption, medical absence,
emergency absence, hospital admission, hospital discharge,
medical certificate, excused absence, Department Head,
Dean approval, make-up exam


================================================================================
TOPIC 8: LIMITATIONS OF MEDICAL ATTENDANCE APPROVAL
================================================================================

Medical or emergency approval may excuse specific absences for the purpose
of attendance or examination eligibility.

However, an approved absence does not automatically remove the student's
academic responsibilities.

Students are still responsible for:

- Covering missed course material.
- Completing assigned academic work.
- Completing required laboratory assessments.
- Following any additional requirements given by the course faculty.


================================================================================
TOPIC 9: ATTENDANCE AND FINAL EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY
================================================================================

A minimum attendance of 80% is required according to the stated attendance
policy for eligibility to sit for examinations.

If a student's attendance falls below 80% without an approved official
justification or exemption, the student may be debarred from taking the
Final Examination for that course.

Severe attendance shortages may result in a UW status according to the
applicable academic rules.


================================================================================
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
================================================================================

Q: What is the minimum attendance requirement at AIUB?

A:
The stated minimum attendance requirement is 80% for each registered course.


Q: Is the 80% attendance requirement applicable to undergraduate students?

A:
Yes. The stated policy applies to undergraduate courses.


Q: Is the 80% attendance requirement applicable to graduate students?

A:
Yes. The stated policy applies to graduate courses as well.


Q: How is attendance percentage calculated?

A:
Attendance percentage is calculated as:

(Number of Classes Attended / Total Number of Classes Held) × 100.


Q: If I attend 16 out of 20 classes, what is my attendance percentage?

A:
Your attendance percentage is 80%.

Calculation:
(16 / 20) × 100 = 80%.


Q: What happens if my attendance is between 70% and 79%?

A:
This is treated as a borderline attendance shortage under the stated
policy. The student may receive warning notifications through VUES.


Q: What happens if my attendance falls below 80%?

A:
Without approved official justification, the student may become ineligible
to sit for the Final Examination in that course.


Q: What happens if my attendance becomes severely low?

A:
If attendance falls severely below the required threshold, typically below
approximately 70%–75%, the university may register a UW (Unofficial
Withdrawal) status according to the applicable rules.


Q: What does UW mean?

A:
UW means Unofficial Withdrawal.


Q: Can I take the Final Examination if I have attendance below 80%?

A:
Without an approved official justification or exemption, a student below
the required attendance threshold may be debarred from taking the Final
Examination.


Q: Can medical problems justify absence from classes?

A:
Severe illness, hospitalization, major surgery, or bereavement in the
immediate family may be reviewed for official approval if valid
documentation is submitted promptly.


Q: How soon should I report a serious illness or emergency?

A:
The stated policy requires the student or authorized guardian to notify
the Department Head and Course Instructor within 3 working days of the
onset of the illness or emergency.


Q: What documents are required for medical absence approval?

A:
Depending on the circumstances, students may need to provide a medical
certificate, hospital admission or discharge papers, or other relevant
official documentation.


Q: Who approves a medical attendance exemption?

A:
The request is subject to verification and approval by the Department Head
and Dean according to the stated procedure.


Q: Does medical approval mean I can ignore missed academic work?

A:
No. Even when an absence is approved, the student remains responsible for
covering missed course material, completing assignments, and completing
required laboratory assessments.


Q: Can an approved medical absence allow a make-up examination?

A:
Depending on the circumstances and applicable rules, an approved medical
or emergency absence may allow the student to complete a make-up evaluation
or examination.
