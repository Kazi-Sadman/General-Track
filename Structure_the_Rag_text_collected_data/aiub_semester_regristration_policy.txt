TOPIC 1: AIUB SEMESTER SYSTEM
=============================

AIUB operates on a Tri-Semester System. There are three regular academic semesters in a calendar year.

1. Spring Semester

* Starts in January.
* Duration: approximately 14–16 weeks.

2. Summer Semester

* Starts in May.
* Duration: approximately 14–16 weeks.

3. Fall Semester

* Starts in September.
* Duration: approximately 14–16 weeks.

Therefore, AIUB has three regular semesters in one academic year:
Spring, Summer, and Fall.

KEYWORDS:
AIUB semester system, three semesters, tri-semester, Spring semester,
Summer semester, Fall semester, semester duration, academic year

TOPIC 2: SEMESTER TERM STRUCTURE
================================

Each regular semester is divided into two academic evaluation terms:

1. Midterm Term

* The first half of the semester.
* Includes midterm academic evaluation and examinations.

2. Final Term

* The second half of the semester.
* Includes final academic evaluation and final examinations.

KEYWORDS:
midterm term, final term, semester evaluation, midterm, final term,
academic evaluation, semester structure

TOPIC 3: REGULAR CREDIT LOAD
============================

Undergraduate Students:

* Standard semester credit load: 12 to 18 Credit Hours.
* This normally corresponds to approximately 4 to 6 courses.

Graduate Students:

* MBA / MS standard semester credit load: 6 to 12 Credit Hours.

Minimum Credit Load:

* Students normally need to register for at least 9 Credit Hours
  in a regular semester to maintain full-time student status.
* An exception may apply during the student's final graduating semester.

KEYWORDS:
credit load, credit hours, undergraduate credit load, graduate credit load,
minimum credits, maximum credits, full-time student, AIUB credits,
semester course load

================================================================================
TOPIC 4: MAXIMUM CREDIT LOAD AND OVERLOAD
=========================================

Students with a CGPA of 3.50 or higher may apply for an overload.

Maximum overload:

* Up to 21 Credit Hours per semester.
* Overload requires approval from the departmental academic advisor.

Therefore:

* Standard undergraduate load: 12–18 credits.
* Overload possibility: up to 21 credits for eligible students with
  CGPA 3.50 or higher and advisor approval.

KEYWORDS:
overload, 21 credits, maximum credit hours, CGPA 3.50,
advisor approval, extra credits, additional course

================================================================================
TOPIC 5: CREDIT LIMIT FOR ACADEMIC PROBATION
============================================

Students on Academic Probation have a restricted semester credit load.

Probationary Credit Limit:

* Maximum approximately 9 to 12 Credit Hours per semester.

Academic Probation therefore limits the number of courses a student
can register for in a semester.

KEYWORDS:
academic probation credits, probation credit limit, 9 credits,
12 credits, restricted course load, probation registration

TOPIC 6: COURSE REGISTRATION THROUGH VUES
=========================================

Course registration is conducted digitally through the VUES
(Virtual University Expert System) portal.

Registration takes place during the designated registration window
published in the official Academic Calendar.

Basic registration process:

1. Check the registration schedule and assigned timeslot.
2. Access the VUES portal.
3. Select eligible courses.
4. Select available course sections and time-slots.
5. Follow the official curriculum flow-chart.
6. Confirm registration by completing the required payment.

Registration timeslots are allocated based on:

* Total earned credits.
* Academic standing.

Students should select courses according to their official curriculum
and academic progression.

KEYWORDS:
VUES registration, course registration, AIUB VUES, registration portal,
registration window, registration timeslot, earned credits,
academic standing, course section, curriculum flow chart

================================================================================
TOPIC 7: REGISTRATION SLOT ALLOCATION
=====================================

AIUB registration timeslots are assigned based on a student's:

* Total earned credit hours.
* Academic standing.

Students receive their designated registration opportunity and then
select available courses and sections through VUES.

KEYWORDS:
registration slot, registration timeslot, earned credits,

TOPIC 8: COURSE PREREQUISITES
=============================

A prerequisite is a foundational course that must be successfully
completed before a student can register for a higher-level course.

Prerequisite Rule:

* The prerequisite course must be passed before registering for the
  dependent/higher-level course.
* VUES automatically checks prerequisite completion.
* A prerequisite must normally be passed with at least a 'D' grade.

If the prerequisite has not been passed with the required minimum grade,
VUES blocks registration for the next course.

KEYWORDS:
course prerequisite, prerequisite course, prerequisite requirement,
VUES prerequisite check, minimum D grade, prerequisite registration,
course dependency

TOPIC 9: PREREQUISITE WAIVER AND CO-REQUISITE REQUEST
=====================================================

Prerequisite waivers or co-requisite requests may be considered only
under exceptional circumstances.

Such requests are primarily applicable to graduating seniors and require
approval from the Department Head.

A prerequisite waiver is therefore not an automatic registration option.

KEYWORDS:
prerequisite waiver, prerequisite exception, co-requisite,
graduating senior, Department Head approval, course waiver

TOPIC 10: REGISTRATION CONFIRMATION AND PAYMENT
===============================================

Course registration is temporary until the registration is confirmed
through the required payment.

To confirm registration:

* Students must pay the required registration deposit or tuition
  installment.
* Payment must be completed by the announced deadline.

If registration is not confirmed through payment by the deadline:

* The registration may be automatically canceled/dropped by the system.
* The student may need to register again during a later registration
  period, subject to availability and applicable rules.

KEYWORDS:
registration confirmation, confirm registration, registration payment,
tuition installment, registration deposit, payment deadline,
unconfirmed registration, course dropped, VUES

TOPIC 11: ADD/DROP WEEK
=======================

Course Add/Drop is allowed during the official Add/Drop period.

Period:

* Normally during the first week of classes.
* The exact dates are specified in the Academic Calendar.

Procedure:

* Courses can be added or dropped online through the VUES portal.

Financial Adjustment:

* Dropping a course during the official Add/Drop window results in
  full financial credit toward other added courses without penalty,
  according to the provided policy.

KEYWORDS:
add drop, add/drop week, course drop, course add,
VUES add drop, first week, academic calendar, financial credit,
course change

================================================================================
TOPIC 12: COURSE WITHDRAWAL — W GRADE
=====================================

After the official Add/Drop deadline, a student may withdraw from a
course during the approved withdrawal period.

Official Withdrawal Period:

* From after the Add/Drop deadline until the announced Midterm
  withdrawal cutoff.

Result:

* The student receives a 'W' (Official Withdrawal) grade on the
  academic transcript.

CGPA Impact:

* A 'W' grade does NOT affect CGPA calculation.

Financial Impact:

* Tuition fees for the withdrawn course are non-refundable.

Academic Honor Impact:

* A 'W' grade disqualifies the student from Dean's List and
  Convocation Graduation Distinction (Honors), according to the
  provided policy.

KEYWORDS:
W grade, withdrawal, official withdrawal, course withdrawal,
midterm withdrawal, W transcript, CGPA W grade, tuition refund,
Dean's List W, graduation honors W

================================================================================
TOPIC 13: DIFFERENCE BETWEEN ADD/DROP AND WITHDRAWAL
====================================================

ADD/DROP:

* Takes place during the official Add/Drop period.
* Normally during the first week of classes.
* Processed through VUES.
* Course can be added or dropped during this period.
* Financial adjustment may apply according to the Add/Drop policy.

WITHDRAWAL:

* Takes place after the Add/Drop deadline.
* Must occur before the announced Midterm withdrawal cutoff.
* Results in a 'W' grade.
* W does not affect CGPA.
* Tuition fees are non-refundable.
* W can affect eligibility for academic honors.

KEYWORDS:
add drop vs withdrawal, difference between W and drop,
course drop, course withdrawal, W grade, Add/Drop deadline,
withdrawal deadline

TOPIC 14: RETAKING FAILED COURSES
=================================

If a student fails a course with an 'F' or 'UW' grade, the student must
retake and pass the course if it is a core or required subject.

Retake Grade Replacement Rule for Undergraduate Students:

* First four (4) retaken 'F' subjects:
  The new grade replaces the previous 'F' in CGPA calculation.

* From the 5th retaken course onward:
  Both the old and new grades are factored into the cumulative average.

This rule applies to the provided undergraduate retake policy.

KEYWORDS:
course retake, failed course, F grade, UW grade, retake F,
grade replacement, first four retakes, fifth retake,
CGPA retake, failed core course

================================================================================
TOPIC 15: REPEATING A PASSED COURSE FOR GRADE IMPROVEMENT
=========================================================

A student may repeat a course that they have already passed for
grade improvement if the earned grade is below C+.

Eligibility:

* Previous grade must be below C+.
* Grade Point must be less than 2.50.

Courses with grades of B or higher cannot be retaken for grade
improvement under the provided policy.

For eligible grade-improvement attempts:

* Both attempts appear on the academic transcript.
* The higher grade is used for CGPA calculation, subject to
  university limits.

KEYWORDS:
grade improvement, repeat course, retake passed course,
improve CGPA, C+ requirement, below C+, grade point 2.50,
B grade, higher grade, repeated course

TOPIC 16: REGISTRATION PAYMENT NOT COMPLETED ON TIME
====================================================

If a student does not complete the required registration payment by
the announced deadline:

* The selected course sections may be automatically dropped from
  the VUES portal.
* The student may need to register again during late registration.
* Re-registration is subject to available seats.
* Late registration fees may apply.

Therefore, students should complete registration confirmation and
payment within the announced deadline.

KEYWORDS:
late registration, missed payment deadline, course dropped,
registration fee, VUES dropped courses, seat availability,
late fee, registration confirmation

TOPIC 17: COMMON REGISTRATION SCENARIOS
=======================================

Scenario 1:
A student wants to register for a course but has not passed its
prerequisite.

Result:
VUES blocks registration because the prerequisite requirement has
not been satisfied.

Scenario 2:
A student has CGPA 3.60 and wants to take additional courses.

Result:
The student may apply for an overload up to 21 credits, subject to
departmental advisor approval.

Scenario 3:
A student is on Academic Probation and wants to register for 18 credits.

Result:
The student is restricted to the probationary credit limit of
approximately 9–12 credits.

Scenario 4:
A student drops a course during the official Add/Drop Week.

Result:
The course can be dropped through VUES during the permitted period,
and the applicable financial adjustment is made according to the
Add/Drop policy.

Scenario 5:
A student withdraws from a course after Add/Drop but before the
Midterm withdrawal deadline.

Result:
The student receives a W grade. The W does not affect CGPA, but the
course tuition is non-refundable and the W can affect honors eligibility.

Scenario 6:
A student fails a required course.

Result:
The student must retake and pass the course if it is a core/required
subject.

Scenario 7:
A student passed a course with a grade below C+ and wants to improve it.

Result:
The student may retake the course for grade improvement under the
provided policy.

Scenario 8:
A student does not confirm registration by paying the required amount
before the deadline.

Result:
The selected registration may be automatically canceled/dropped,
and the student may need to re-register during late registration,
subject to seat availability and applicable fees.

TOPIC 18: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
====================================

Q: How many semesters are there in a year at AIUB?
A: AIUB follows a tri-semester system with three regular semesters:
Spring, Summer, and Fall.

Q: When does the Spring semester start?
A: The Spring semester starts in January.

Q: When does the Summer semester start?
A: The Summer semester starts in May.

Q: When does the Fall semester start?
A: The Fall semester starts in September.

Q: How many credits can an undergraduate student normally take?
A: The standard undergraduate credit load is 12 to 18 Credit Hours
per semester.

Q: Can I take more than 18 credits?
A: Students with a CGPA of 3.50 or higher may apply for an overload
of up to 21 Credit Hours, subject to departmental advisor approval.

Q: How many credits can a student on Academic Probation take?
A: Students on Academic Probation are restricted to approximately
9 to 12 Credit Hours per semester.

Q: Where do I register for courses?
A: Course registration is conducted online through the VUES portal.

Q: Why can't I register for a course?
A: One common reason is that the required prerequisite course has not
been passed with the required minimum grade.

Q: What is the minimum grade required to satisfy a prerequisite?
A: The provided policy states that the prerequisite must be passed
with at least a D grade.

Q: Can a prerequisite be waived?
A: A prerequisite or co-requisite waiver may be considered under
exceptional circumstances for graduating seniors, subject to
Department Head approval.

Q: What is Add/Drop?
A: Add/Drop is the official period, normally during the first week
of classes, when students can add or drop courses through VUES.

Q: What happens if I withdraw from a course after Add/Drop?
A: You receive a W grade if the withdrawal is within the approved
withdrawal period.

Q: Does W affect CGPA?
A: No. A W grade does not affect CGPA according to the provided policy.

Q: Are tuition fees refunded after withdrawing from a course?
A: No. Tuition fees for a withdrawn course are non-refundable.

Q: Can I retake a failed course?
A: Yes. If a student fails a core or required course with F or UW,
the course must be retaken and passed.

Q: How are the first four retaken F courses treated in CGPA?
A: For the first four retaken F subjects in an undergraduate program,
the new grade replaces the F in CGPA calculation.

Q: What happens from the 5th retaken F course onward?
A: From the 5th retaken course onward, both the old and new grades
are factored into the CGPA.

Q: Can I retake a course that I already passed?
A: Yes, if the previous grade is below C+ (Grade Point below 2.50),
subject to the applicable university rules.

Q: Can I retake a course where I received B or higher?
A: According to the provided policy, courses with B or higher cannot
be retaken for grade improvement.

Q: What happens if I do not pay the registration fee on time?
A: The selected course sections may be automatically dropped from
VUES. The student may need to re-register during late registration,
subject to seat availability and applicable late fees.
