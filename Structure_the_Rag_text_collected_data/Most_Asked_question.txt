6. INCOMPLETE (I) GRADE
-----------------------

An I grade is assigned when a student does not complete course requirements despite having passing academic performance and required attendance.

Requirements may include:
- Quizzes
- Assignments
- Term examinations

Deadline:
The maximum deadline to complete the requirements is four weeks after the semester ends or according to the Academic Calendar.

If the student does not complete the requirements within the deadline, the I grade becomes F.

A student may apply to drop the course within the deadline.

Students under academic probation may have restrictions on dropping courses.

A course with an F grade cannot be dropped.

Students cannot complete final registration for the next semester if they have an unresolved I grade from the previous semester.


7. UNOFFICIAL WITHDRAWAL (UW)
----------------------------

A UW grade is assigned when a student cannot meet course requirements and cannot pass the course.

Reasons may include:
- Attendance below the required level
- Not appearing in quizzes
- Not appearing in term examinations
- Not submitting assignments

Students may apply to drop the course within the allowed deadline.

If the student does not submit a drop application within the deadline, UW becomes F.

A course with an F grade cannot be dropped.


8. OFFICIAL WITHDRAWAL (W)
--------------------------

A W grade is given when a student's course drop request is successfully processed and approved.

Required approvals may include:
- Course Teacher
- Department Head/Director/Dean
- Registrar's Office


9. FAIL (F) GRADE
-----------------

An F grade is assigned when a student completes the required course components but fails to achieve the minimum passing marks.

Retake:
The student must retake the course in a future semester, preferably the next semester.

Grade Replacement:
For the first four UG retaken courses and first two PG retaken courses, the F grade becomes non-credited after the course is retaken.

From the fifth UG retaken course and third PG retaken course onward, both the F grade and the new grade are considered in CGPA calculation.

Courses with an F grade cannot be dropped.


10. COURSE DROP POLICY
----------------------

Drop applications must be submitted online through the AIUB portal.

During the semester:
Approval is required from the course teacher.

After the semester and before the deadline:
Approval is required from the department head/director/dean.

Finalization:
The Registrar's Office finalizes the approved drop request.

Deadline:
The final deadline is four weeks after the semester ends or according to the Academic Calendar.

Courses with an F grade cannot be dropped.

Students under academic probation have restrictions on course dropping.


11. CLASS ATTENDANCE
--------------------

Students must attend at least 80% of classes for each course in a semester.

Attendance is a mandatory component of the course.

Absence exceeding 20% may result in:
- Unofficial Withdrawal (UW)
- Other actions according to university policy


12. GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS
---------------------------

Minimum CGPA for Graduation:
2.50 out of 4.00

Students must complete their degree within a maximum period of 1.5 times the normal duration of the degree program.

Extension may be granted for valid reasons at the discretion of the Vice Chancellor.

CGPA Requirements:
1st-11th Convocation: 2.50
12th-16th Convocation: 2.75
17th Convocation onwards: 2.50

Exception:
Students with specified older ID ranges were allowed to graduate under the previous 2.50 CGPA requirement during the 12th-16th Convocation period.


13. ACADEMIC PROBATION
----------------------

If a student's CGPA falls below 2.50 out of 4.00, the student is placed under academic probation.

During probation:
- Students receive counseling and guidance.
- Students receive a warning.
- Students receive a deadline to improve their CGPA.

If the student fails to recover the required CGPA, admission may be cancelled and the student may be terminated from the university.

After termination, the student cannot apply for readmission.
