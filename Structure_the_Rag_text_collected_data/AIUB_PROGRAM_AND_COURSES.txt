1. PROGRAMS AND COURSES
-----------------------

American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB) offers undergraduate and graduate/postgraduate degree programs through five faculties.

Doctoral Programs:
AIUB does not currently offer independent doctoral (PhD) degree programs.


UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS
---------------------

Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASS):
- BA in English
- BA in Media and Mass Communication
- LL.B.
- BSS in Economics

Faculty of Business Administration (FBA):
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA)

Faculty of Engineering (FE):
- B.Sc. in Computer Engineering (CoE)
- B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)
- B.Sc. in Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE)

Faculty of Health and Life Sciences (FHLS):
- B.Sc. in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology (BMB)
- Bachelor of Pharmacy (B.Pharm.)

Faculty of Science and Technology (FST):
- B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)
- B.Sc. in Computer Network and Cyber Security (CNCS)
- B.Sc. in Data Science
- Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.)


GRADUATE / POSTGRADUATE PROGRAMS
--------------------------------

Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASS):

- LL.M.

Faculty of Business Administration (FBA):
