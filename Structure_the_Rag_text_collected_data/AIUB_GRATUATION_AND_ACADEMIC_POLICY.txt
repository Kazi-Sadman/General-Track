================================================================================
AIUB GRADUATION REQUIREMENTS & ACADEMIC STANDING POLICY
================================================================================

TOPIC: Minimum CGPA Required for Graduation

- Undergraduate students must achieve a minimum CGPA of 2.50 on a 4.00 scale to graduate.
- Graduate students must achieve a minimum CGPA of 2.50 on a 4.00 scale to graduate.
- Passing individual courses does not guarantee graduation.
- A student with passing grades in individual courses still cannot graduate if the overall CGPA is below 2.50.

TOPIC: Total Credit Requirements by Program

Students must successfully complete all prescribed credit hours in their respective degree program before receiving an AIUB degree.

Faculty of Science and Technology (FST):
- B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering (CSE): 140–148 credit hours.
- B.Sc. in Software Engineering (SE): 140–144 credit hours.

Faculty of Engineering (FE):
- B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE): 148 credit hours.
- B.Sc. in Computer Engineering (CoE): 144–148 credit hours.

Faculty of Business Administration (FBA):
- Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA): 126–130 credit hours.
- Master of Business Administration (MBA): 36–60 credit hours depending on waiver and academic background.

Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences (FASS):
- LL.B. (Honours): 130–135 credit hours.
- B.A. in English, B.S.S. in Economics, and MMC: 120–130 credit hours.

TOPIC: Degree Completion and Required Courses

To receive a degree, students must complete all required courses and credit components specified in their program curriculum.

University Core / General Education (GED):
- English Writing and Oral Communication.
- Bangladesh Studies.
- Computer Fundamentals.
- Mathematics.
- Basic Science and Social Science courses.

Program Core Courses:
- Mandatory foundational and advanced courses related to the student's major.

Major and Elective Concentration Courses:
- Students must complete the required elective or concentration credits within their selected specialization.

Residency Requirement:
- Transfer students must complete the required percentage of their total program credits directly at AIUB.
- The stated residency requirement is approximately 40%–60% of total program credits, depending on applicable program rules.

TOPIC: Thesis and Capstone Project Requirements

Thesis/Capstone Eligibility:
- Students generally need to complete a minimum credit threshold before registering for Research Methodology, Capstone, or Thesis I.
- For undergraduate programs, the stated threshold is typically 100+ completed credits.

Thesis/Capstone Structure:
- Thesis or Capstone is generally completed over two consecutive terms.
- The two stages may be referred to as Capstone Project I and Capstone Project II.
- Students must work under the supervision of assigned faculty members.

Thesis/Capstone Defense:
- A formal oral presentation and defense is required.
- The defense is conducted before the assigned faculty board or committee.
- Students must submit the final thesis/project report.
- Depending on the program, source code, prototype, hardware model, or other project materials may also be required.

Thesis/Capstone Failure:
- A failing grade in Thesis or Capstone prevents graduation until the required course/project is successfully retaken and defended.

TOPIC: Internship and Practicum Requirements

Internship Eligibility:
- Internship or practicum is generally offered during the final stage of the undergraduate program.
- Students must complete the minimum required number of credits before taking the internship.
- The stated typical requirement is approximately 130–140 completed credits for undergraduate programs.

Internship Duration:
- The standard internship duration is approximately 12 weeks or 3 months.
- The placement should be completed at an approved corporate, industrial, or institutional organization.

Internship Deliverables:
- Students must submit periodic progress reports, such as bi-weekly or monthly reports.
- Progress reports should be certified by the industry supervisor where required.
- A comprehensive Final Internship Report is required.
- Students must complete a Final Internship Oral Defense or Viva-Voce.
- The final internship assessment is conducted by the departmental board according to applicable requirements.


================================================================================
TOPIC: Academic Standing and Good Academic Standing

Good Academic Standing:
- A student is considered to be in good academic standing when the CGPA is 2.50 or above.
- The minimum CGPA required for graduation is also 2.50.

Academic Probation:
- Students whose CGPA falls below the applicable probation threshold may be placed on Academic Probation.
- The stated threshold may be below 2.00 or below 2.50 depending on the student's program stage and applicable academic rules.

Probation Credit Restriction:
- Students on Academic Probation may be restricted to a lower course load.
- The stated typical maximum is approximately 9–12 credits in a regular semester.
- The purpose of the reduced credit load is to allow students to focus on improving academic performance and retaking low-grade courses.

Probation Advising:
- Students on Academic Probation may be required to meet regularly with the designated Probationary Advising Office or academic advisor.

Academic Dismissal:
- If a student fails to improve the required CGPA after the specified probationary semesters, academic dismissal may occur.
- The stated rule indicates that failure to raise CGPA to 2.00 or above after two consecutive probationary semesters can result in academic dismissal.


================================================================================
TOPIC: Graduation Application Process

After completing all required credits and courses, students must complete the graduation application and clearance process.

Step 1 — Graduation Application:
- Students submit an online Graduation Application through the VUES portal.
- The application must be submitted during the announced graduation or convocation application window.

Step 2 — Departmental and Academic Audit:
- The student's completed courses and degree requirements are reviewed.
- The Department Head and Registrar or relevant academic authority verify degree completion.

Step 3 — Financial and Library Clearance:
- Students must clear all outstanding financial dues with the Accounts Department.
- Students must return outstanding library books.
- Students must settle applicable library fines.
- Students must complete required proctorial and laboratory equipment clearance.

Step 4 — Certificate and Transcript:
- Final certificate and transcript release occur after the required academic approvals.
- The degree is officially awarded through the university's graduation/convocation process.


================================================================================
TOPIC: Graduation with F or I Grade

- Students cannot normally apply for graduation while required courses remain failed or incomplete.
- All required courses must be successfully completed.
- An F grade in a required course must be resolved according to the applicable retake policy before graduation.
- An I (Incomplete) grade must be cleared before completing the graduation requirements.
- Mandatory subjects with unresolved F or I grades must be successfully completed before applying for graduation.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS: GRADUATION
================================================================================

Q: What CGPA is required for graduation?

A:
A minimum CGPA of 2.50 on a 4.00 scale is required for undergraduate and graduate graduation.

Q: Can I graduate with a CGPA below 2.50 if I passed all my courses?

A:
No. Passing individual courses does not guarantee graduation. The required cumulative CGPA must also be achieved.

Q: How many credits are required for CSE graduation?

A:
The stated CSE requirement is 140–148 credit hours, depending on the applicable CSE curriculum.

Q: How many credits are required for BBA?

A:
The stated BBA requirement is 126–130 credit hours.

Q: How many credits are required for EEE?

A:
The stated EEE requirement is 148 credit hours.

Q: How many credits are required for Software Engineering?

A:
The stated Software Engineering requirement is 140–144 credit hours.

Q: How many credits are required for Computer Engineering?

A:
The stated Computer Engineering requirement is 144–148 credit hours.

Q: How many credits are required for LL.B.?

A:
The stated LL.B. (Honours) requirement is 130–135 credit hours.

Q: Can I graduate with an F grade?

A:
Not if the F remains unresolved in a required course. The required course must be successfully completed according to the applicable retake rules before graduation.

Q: Can I graduate with an I grade?

A:
An unresolved I (Incomplete) grade must be cleared before graduation requirements are completed.

Q: When can I register for Thesis or Capstone?

A:
Students generally need to complete a minimum credit threshold before registering for Research Methodology or Thesis/Capstone I. The stated typical undergraduate threshold is 100+ completed credits.

Q: How long is the internship?

A:
The stated standard internship duration is approximately 12 weeks or 3 months.

Q: What happens if my CGPA is below the academic standing requirement?

A:
Depending on the applicable academic rules and program stage, the student may be placed on Academic Probation and may face restrictions on course load.

Q: How many credits can a probation student take?

A:
The stated typical probationary course-load restriction is approximately 9–12 credits per regular semester.

Q: What happens after two consecutive probation semesters?

A:
The stated policy indicates that failure to raise the CGPA to the required level after two consecutive probationary semesters may result in academic dismissal.
