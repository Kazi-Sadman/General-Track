AIUB EXAMINATION RULES, ACADEMIC ASSESSMENT & GRADE REGULATIONS

TOPIC: General Assessment Overview

Every academic semester at AIUB includes continuous assessment components such as quizzes, midterm examinations, final examinations, assignments, lab work, presentations, and attendance.

Quizzes:
- Quizzes are conducted periodically during the Midterm and Final terms.
- Typically, 2 to 3 quizzes are conducted per term depending on the course credit load.
- Missed quizzes generally cannot be retaken unless the course instructor explicitly authorizes a retake due to extraordinary verified circumstances.

Midterm Examination:
- The Midterm Examination is conducted around the middle of the semester according to the official Academic Calendar.
- It generally covers the syllabus assigned for the first half of the semester.
- The typical duration is 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours.

Final Examination:
- The Final Examination is conducted at the end of the semester.
- It covers the remaining or comprehensive syllabus.
- Students must meet the required attendance and financial clearance requirements to take the Final Examination.
- The typical duration is 1 hour 30 minutes to 2 hours.


TOPIC: Examination Eligibility and Exam Rules

Exam Permit:
- AIUB follows a "No Permit, No Exam" policy.
- Students must download or collect the official Exam Permit from the VUES portal.
- The Exam Permit must be signed by the invigilator during the examination.

Attendance and Financial Clearance:
- Students must maintain the required minimum attendance, normally around 75% to 80%.
- Semester tuition fees and other account dues must be cleared before Exam Permit generation.

Exam-Day Conduct:
- Students must visibly wear their official AIUB Student ID Card during the examination.
- Students should arrive at least 15 minutes before the scheduled examination time.
- Students arriving more than 1 hour late will not be permitted to take the examination.
- Students are not allowed to leave the examination room while the examination is ongoing.
- Only simple scientific calculators are allowed.
- Programmable calculators, smartwatches, mobile phones, and other digital devices are prohibited.
- Mobile phones must be switched off and placed at the designated front area of the examination hall.


TOPIC: Make-Up Examination and Incomplete Grade

Make-Up Examination Eligibility:
- A Make-Up Examination may be allowed only under valid, documented, and extreme emergency conditions.
- Examples include severe medical emergencies, hospitalization, or family bereavement.
- A Make-Up Examination is not guaranteed.
- Students must submit a formal written application and obtain the required approval from the Head of Department or Coordinator and Dean.

Missing a Final Examination:
- Missing a Final Examination without an approved official justification may result in a zero for the final examination or an automatic F/UW grade for the course.
- If a student has a valid documented reason, such as medical hospitalization, and submits the documentation promptly before grade submission, an I (Incomplete) grade may be assigned temporarily subject to approval.
- An I grade must be cleared by taking a Make-Up Examination within the deadline specified in the Academic Calendar.
- The Make-Up Examination deadline is generally within the first few weeks of the following semester.
- If an I grade is not resolved within the required timeframe, it automatically converts to an F.
- Students with an unresolved I grade cannot complete final registration for subsequent semesters.


TOPIC: Grade Improvement and Course Retaking

Improving a Passing Grade:
- A student who receives a passing grade with a GPA below 2.50, such as C or D, may be eligible to retake the course to improve the CGPA.
- Grade improvement is subject to department approval and course availability.
- Grade improvement is generally not available for A or B grades.

Retaking Failed Courses:
- Students receiving F, I, W, or UW grades must retake the course according to the applicable academic regulations.
- A student is allowed to retake a specific course only once.

Undergraduate F-Grade Replacement:
- For undergraduate students, the first four retaken courses with an F grade have the previous F treated as non-credited after the course is retaken.
- Starting from the fifth retaken F course, both the previous F and the new grade are included in CGPA calculations.

Graduate F-Grade Replacement:
- For graduate students, the first two retaken courses with an F grade have the previous F treated as non-credited after retaking.
- Starting from the third retaken F course, both the previous F and the new grade are included in CGPA calculations.


TOPIC: Honors and Graduation Eligibility

- Students who retake any course or receive an F, W, or UW grade become ineligible for Academic Honors and Dean's List recognition.
- Academic Honors include distinctions such as Summa Cum Laude, Magna Cum Laude, and Cum Laude.


TOPIC: Examination Misconduct and Academic Dishonesty

AIUB maintains strict rules against academic dishonesty and examination misconduct.

Examples of Examination Misconduct:
- Copying or attempting to copy from another student.
- Using unauthorized materials during an examination.
- Bringing cheat sheets or unauthorized notes into the examination hall.
- Bringing unauthorized electronic devices such as mobile phones or smart devices.
- Impersonating another student during an examination.
- Communicating with other students during the examination.
- Tampering with answer scripts or Exam Permits.

Possible Disciplinary Measures:
- Immediate removal from the examination hall.
- Cancellation of the examination.
- F or FD (Disciplinary Fail) grade for the course.
- Suspension from the university for one or more semesters.
- Permanent expulsion from AIUB for severe or repeated offenses.
- An FD grade awarded by the Disciplinary Committee cannot be replaced, retaken, or removed from the transcript.
- Students with disciplinary records lose eligibility for university scholarships, tuition waivers, and academic awards.


TOPIC: Frequently Asked Questions About Examinations

Question: Who should I contact if I miss an examination because of illness?
Answer: Contact the course teacher, department head, and Controller of Examinations office immediately. Provide medical documentation and apply for an I grade or Make-Up Examination according to the applicable procedure.

Question: What happens if I forget my Exam Permit?
Answer: A student may not be allowed into the examination hall without the required Exam Permit. The student should print a replacement permit from the VUES portal or contact the relevant IT/Accounts desk before entering the examination hall.

Question: Can I improve an A grade?
Answer: Generally, grade improvement is restricted to passing grades below a 2.50 GPA, such as C or D. A grades are generally not eligible for improvement.

Question: Can I improve a B grade?
Answer: Generally, grade improvement is restricted to passing grades below a 2.50 GPA, such as C or D. B grades are generally not eligible for improvement.

Question: What attendance is required for examinations?
Answer: The stated normal minimum attendance threshold is around 75% to 80%, subject to the applicable academic rules.

Question: Can I take a Make-Up Examination whenever I want?
Answer: No. Make-Up Examinations are only considered under valid, documented, and extreme emergency circumstances and require the necessary approval.

Question: What happens if I have an unresolved I grade?
Answer: An unresolved I grade must be cleared within the specified deadline. Otherwise, it automatically converts to an F, and the student cannot complete final registration for subsequent semesters while the I remains unresolved.

Question: Are mobile phones allowed in the examination hall?
Answer: No. Mobile phones and other unauthorized digital devices are prohibited during examinations. Mobile phones must be switched off and placed at the designated front area of the examination hall.

Question: Are programmable calculators allowed?
Answer: No. Only simple scientific calculators are allowed. Programmable calculators are prohibited.

Question: How late can a student arrive for an examination?
Answer: Students arriving more than 1 hour late will not be permitted to take the examination.
