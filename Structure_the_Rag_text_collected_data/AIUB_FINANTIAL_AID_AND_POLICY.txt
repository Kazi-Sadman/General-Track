AIUB FINANCIAL AID POLICY

University: American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)

Purpose:
Financial Aid is provided to help deserving AIUB students manage education costs and financial difficulties.

Financial Aid categories:
1. General Need Based Tuition Fee Waiver
2. Freedom Fighter Waiver
3. Waiver for July Injured
4. Alumni Tuition Fee Waiver
5. Corporate Tuition Fee Waiver
6. Sibling Waiver
7. AIUB Employee Waiver
8. AIUB Employee Dependent Waiver
9. On-Campus Jobs
10. Volunteer Scheme


==================================================
GENERAL NEED BASED TUITION FEE WAIVER
==================================================

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Must complete two regular semesters (Spring and Fall).
2. Can apply only in a regular semester.
3. Fresh applications are not allowed for Summer semester.
4. Concerned department will arrange VIVA for deserving candidates after application submission.
5. Number of recipients may vary depending on available slots.
6. Undergraduate students must take 14-15 credits per regular semester.
7. Graduate students must take 12 credits per regular semester.
8. EMBA students must take 4 courses per regular semester.
9. Must have CGPA 3.50 or above.
10. No F (Fail).
11. No I (Incomplete).
12. No W (Withdrawn).
13. No UW (Unofficial Withdrawn).
14. Must not drop any course.
15. Must not retake any course.
16. Must have no disciplinary action.

Decision Authority:
The Scholarship Committee has full authority to make decisions regarding this financial aid.

==================================================
CORPORATE TUITION FEE WAIVER
==================================================

Eligible Students:
Postgraduate students working in public or reputed private organizations.

Required Document:
Original employment document must be submitted every semester at registration.

Eligible Organizations:

Public Organizations:
1. Civil organizations.
2. Defense organizations.

Private Organizations:
1. Individual.
2. Group.

MOU:
Organizations having a valid MOU with AIUB.

Waiver Percentage:
The Scholarship Committee decides the waiver percentage, or the waiver follows the valid MOU between the organization and AIUB.

Minimum Registration:
1. Postgraduate students must register for at least 12 credits every semester.
2. EMBA students must register for at least 4 courses every semester.

Important:
AIUB Management reserves the right to update or cancel an MOU when necessary.


==================================================
SIBLING WAIVER
==================================================

Eligibility:
1. Sibling of a current AIUB student may receive a tuition fee waiver.
2. Siblings must be registered in the same semester.
3. The waiver continues only while both siblings remain registered students.

Waiver Percentage:
The Scholarship Committee decides the percentage of tuition fee waiver.


==================================================
AIUB EMPLOYEE WAIVER
==================================================

Eligibility:
AIUB employees are entitled to tuition fee waiver after one year of full-time service.

Waiver Percentage:
The percentage depends on the number of years the employee has served AIUB.

Decision Authority:
The Scholarship Committee decides the applicable percentage.


==================================================
AIUB EMPLOYEE DEPENDENT WAIVER
==================================================

Eligible Dependents:
First-degree affinity of AIUB employees.

Eligibility:
1. AIUB employee must complete one year of full-time service.

Waiver Percentage:
The percentage depends on the number of years the employee has served AIUB.

Decision Authority:
The Scholarship Committee decides the applicable percentage.

==================================================
VOLUNTEER SCHEME
==================================================

Responsible Office:
Office of Student Affairs (OSA).

Process:
1. OSA maintains a pool of student volunteers.
2. Students are selected through a selection process.
3. Volunteers participate in various university events.
4. Engagement is for a specific period.

Compensation:
Most volunteer activities are completely voluntary.

For some specific events, a nominal compensation may be provided as a daily allowance.


==================================================
FINANCIAL AID RENEWAL
==================================================

General Renewal Requirements:
Recipients of tuition fee waivers must consistently comply with the applicable rules every semester.

Requirements:
1. Undergraduate students must take 14-15 credits per regular semester.
2. Graduate students must take 12 credits per regular semester.
3. Must be enrolled in all academic courses during preceding and subsequent semesters.
4. Must have no disciplinary record.
5. Must maintain the CGPA required for the respective category.
6. Must have no F (Fail).
7. Must have no I (Incomplete).
8. Must have no W (Withdrawn).
9. Must have no UW (Unofficial Withdrawn).
10. Must not drop courses.
11. Must not retake courses.
12. Must maintain attendance above 80%.

Important:
If a student is eligible for more than one scholarship or discount, only one scholarship or discount will apply. The higher scholarship or discount will be applicable.


==================================================
PRIVATE UNIVERSITY ACT GUIDELINES
==================================================

Minimum Scholarship Requirement:
At least 6% of the total student population must receive full scholarship.

Distribution:
1. Freedom Fighter category scholarship recipients must be at least 3% of the total student population.
2. Other categories must cover at least another 3% of the total student population.
