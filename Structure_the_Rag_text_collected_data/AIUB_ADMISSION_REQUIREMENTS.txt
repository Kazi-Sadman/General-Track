26. ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------

Application Form Fee:
BDT 1,000
The application form fee is non-refundable.


UNDERGRADUATE PROGRAMS
----------------------

1. National Curriculum (SSC and HSC)

Engineering, Architecture and Computer Science:
Programs: EEE, CoE, IPE, Architecture, CSE

Requirements:
- Combined GPA of at least 5.00 out of 10.00 in SSC and HSC.
- Minimum GPA of 2.50 in both SSC and HSC.
- Mathematics is required at HSC level for Science and Engineering programs.


Business Administration and Arts/Social Science:
Programs: BBA, English, LLB, MMC, Economics

Requirements:
- Combined GPA of at least 5.00 in SSC and HSC.
- Minimum GPA of 2.50 in both SSC and HSC.


Bachelor of Pharmacy (BPharm):

Requirements:
- Combined GPA of at least 8.00 in SSC and HSC.
- Minimum GPA of 3.50 in both SSC and HSC.
- Minimum GPA of 3.50 in HSC Chemistry.
- Minimum GPA of 3.50 in HSC Biology.
- Minimum GPA of 3.00 in Physics.
- Minimum GPA of 3.00 in Mathematics.


2. English Medium and International Certificates

O-Level:
- Minimum GPA of 2.50 in at least 5 subjects.

A-Level:
- Minimum GPA of 2.00 in at least 2 subjects.

Grade Requirements:
- Minimum C grade is required in O-Level and A-Level subjects.
- For Engineering and Science programs, no grade lower than D is allowed in the best 5 O-Level subjects and best 2 A-Level subjects.
- For BPharm, minimum B grade is recommended in Chemistry and Biology.
- For BPharm, minimum C grade is recommended in Physics and Mathematics.


International Baccalaureate (IB) and U.S. High School Diploma:
Applicants with IB or U.S. High School Diploma may apply subject to the applicable AIUB admission requirements.


GED:
GED results are not accepted for major undergraduate programs at AIUB.


GRADUATE PROGRAMS
-----------------

Programs:
- MBA
- MSCS
- LLM
- MA

Bachelor's Degree Requirement:
- Minimum 3-year or 4-year Bachelor's degree.
- Minimum CGPA of 2.50 out of 4.00.


Previous Academic Requirements:
- Minimum 2nd division or GPA of 2.50 in SSC.
- Minimum 2nd division or GPA of 2.50 in HSC.


EXECUTIVE MBA (EMBA)
--------------------

Academic Requirement:
Applicants must meet the applicable graduate admission requirements.

Work Experience:
- Minimum 3 years of professional work experience.
- Work experience should generally be at managerial or supervisory level.


TRANSCRIPT AND DOCUMENT SUBMISSION
----------------------------------

Applicants must submit official or attested academic documents during the admission process.

Required Documents:
- Attested photocopies of board mark sheets.
- Academic transcripts.
- Academic certificates/testimonials.

International or Foreign-Language Transcripts:
- A certified English translation is required according to AIUB requirements.


HOW TO APPLY
------------

Step 01:
Go to the AIUB Admission Portal:
http://admission.aiub.edu

Create a new applicant account or log in using an existing account.

Step 02:
Complete all required fields with correct information.

Upload:
- Recent color photograph.
- Required academic documents.
- Documents must be uploaded in JPG format where applicable.

Step 03:
Print the Applicant Copy and preserve it for future reference.

Step 04:
Educational information is usually verified within 24 hours.
Verification may take up to 72 hours during weekends.

Step 05:
After verification, the applicant can proceed with the required payment.

Step 06:
Download the Admit Card from the admission portal after verification.

Step 07:
Bring the Admit Card to the Admission Examination.
