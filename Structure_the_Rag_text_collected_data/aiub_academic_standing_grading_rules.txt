TOPIC 1: AIUB GRADING SYSTEM AND GRADE POINT EQUIVALENTS
================================================================================

AIUB uses a 4.00 letter-grading scale to measure academic performance.

Grade conversion:

A+:
- Marks: 90%–100%
- Grade Point: 4.00
- Remark: Outstanding

A:
- Marks: 85%–89%
- Grade Point: 3.75
- Remark: Excellent

B+:
- Marks: 80%–84%
- Grade Point: 3.50
- Remark: Very Good

B:
- Marks: 75%–79%
- Grade Point: 3.25
- Remark: Good

C+:
- Marks: 70%–74%
- Grade Point: 3.00
- Remark: Satisfactory

C:
- Marks: 65%–69%
- Grade Point: 2.75
- Remark: Above Average

D+:
- Marks: 60%–64%
- Grade Point: 2.50
- Remark: Average

D:
- Marks: 50%–59%
- Grade Point: 2.25
- Remark: Pass / Below Average

F:
- Marks: Below 50%
- Grade Point: 0.00
- Remark: Fail


KEYWORDS:
grading system, grade, letter grade, grade point, GP,
A+, A, B+, B, C+, C, D+, D, F, marks, percentage,
4.00 scale, grade conversion

TOPIC 2: NON-GPA ADMINISTRATIVE GRADES
================================================================================

AIUB uses several administrative grades that are different from normal
letter grades.

I — Incomplete:
- Temporary academic status.
- May be assigned for an approved missed Final Examination or incomplete
  academic work.

W — Official Withdrawal:
- Indicates an officially approved course withdrawal within the permitted
  deadline.

UW — Unofficial Withdrawal:
- Indicates an unofficial course withdrawal, including withdrawal resulting
  from attendance shortage according to the applicable rules.

FD — Disciplinary Fail:
- Indicates failure resulting from academic dishonesty, cheating, or
  serious examination misconduct.


KEYWORDS:
I grade, incomplete, W, withdrawal, official withdrawal,
UW, unofficial withdrawal, FD, disciplinary fail,
administrative grade

TOPIC 3: TERM GPA CALCULATION
================================================================================

Term GPA is calculated at the end of each semester.

The formula is:

GPA =
Sum of (Course Credit Hours × Grade Points Earned)
--------------------------------------------------
Sum of Total Credits Attempted in the Term

Example:

If a student takes multiple courses with different credit hours and grade
points, each course's credit hours are multiplied by its grade point.
The resulting quality points are added together and divided by the total
credits attempted during the term.


KEYWORDS:
GPA calculation, term GPA, semester GPA, grade point average,
quality points, credit hours, grade points


================================================================================
TOPIC 4: CUMULATIVE GPA (CGPA) CALCULATION
================================================================================

CGPA represents the student's overall grade point average across completed
academic semesters.

The stated formula is:

CGPA =
Total Quality Points Earned Across All Terms
--------------------------------------------
Total Passed / Attempted Credit Hours

CGPA reflects the student's cumulative academic performance across
multiple semesters.


KEYWORDS:
CGPA calculation, cumulative GPA, cumulative grade point average,
quality points, total credits, semester GPA, overall GPA


================================================================================
TOPIC 5: EFFECT OF NON-NUMERIC GRADES ON CGPA
================================================================================

The stated policy specifies the following treatment of administrative
grades:

W Grade:
- Does not affect CGPA calculation.

I Grade:
- Does not affect CGPA calculation while it remains an Incomplete status.

F Grade:
- Carries 0.00 Grade Point.
- Is included in CGPA calculation according to the applicable retake rules.

UW Grade:
- Carries 0.00 Grade Point.
- Is included in CGPA calculation according to the applicable retake rules.

FD Grade:
- Carries 0.00 Grade Point.
- Is factored into CGPA according to the applicable academic and
  disciplinary rules.


KEYWORDS:
W CGPA, I CGPA, F CGPA, UW CGPA, FD CGPA,
non-GPA grade, grade impact, CGPA effect

TOPIC 6: ACADEMIC STANDING — GOOD STANDING
================================================================================

A student is considered to be in Good Academic Standing when the student's
CGPA is 2.50 or above.

The stated Good Standing requirement applies to both:

- Undergraduate students
- Graduate students


KEYWORDS:
good academic standing, good standing, CGPA 2.50,
academic status, undergraduate standing, graduate standing


================================================================================
TOPIC 7: ACADEMIC WARNING
================================================================================

Academic Warning may be assigned when a student's academic performance
falls into the specified warning range.

The stated policy defines the warning range as:

- Semester GPA or overall CGPA between 2.00 and 2.49.

Academic Warning serves as a formal notice that the student's academic
performance needs improvement.

The student should improve academic performance to avoid Academic
Probation and to meet the 2.50 graduation requirement.


KEYWORDS:
academic warning, warning, GPA 2.00, GPA 2.49,
CGPA 2.00, CGPA 2.49, academic performance


================================================================================
TOPIC 8: ACADEMIC PROBATION
================================================================================

Academic Probation may be initiated when:

- A student's CGPA falls below 2.00; or
- A student remains consistently below the applicable 2.50 academic
  standard beyond the relevant stage of the degree.

Students on Academic Probation may face additional academic restrictions.


KEYWORDS:
academic probation, probation, CGPA below 2.00,
probation threshold, low CGPA, academic status


================================================================================
TOPIC 9: ACADEMIC PROBATION RESTRICTIONS
================================================================================

Students on Academic Probation may be subject to the following restrictions:

1. Maximum Credit Limit:
   - Course registration may be restricted to approximately 9–12 credit
     hours per term.

2. Mandatory Advising:
   - Students may be required to attend regular sessions with the
     Probation Advising Officer.

3. Student Leadership and Clubs:
   - Students on probation may be ineligible to hold office in AIUB
     clubs or associations.

4. Waiver and Scholarship Forfeiture:
   - The stated policy indicates immediate loss of merit-based tuition
     waivers and scholarships while under the applicable probation status.


KEYWORDS:
probation credit limit, 9 credits, 12 credits,
probation advising, academic advisor,
student clubs, student leadership,
tuition waiver, scholarship, probation restriction


================================================================================
TOPIC 10: ACADEMIC DISMISSAL
================================================================================

A student who remains on Academic Probation for two consecutive semesters
without raising the CGPA above 2.00 may be subject to Academic Dismissal
from the university.

Academic dismissal means termination of the student's academic enrollment
under the applicable university regulations.

Readmission or Appeal:
- A dismissed student may seek readmission or appeal.
- The stated policy requires formal approval from the Academic Review
  Committee and the Dean of the respective faculty.


KEYWORDS:
academic dismissal, dismissal, probation two semesters,
two consecutive semesters, CGPA 2.00,
readmission, academic appeal, Academic Review Committee, Dean

TOPIC 11: DISCIPLINARY SUSPENSION AND EXPULSION
================================================================================

Students involved in serious academic misconduct, examination cheating,
plagiarism, or violations of the university code of conduct may face
disciplinary action.

Possible disciplinary consequences include:

- FD (Disciplinary Fail) grade.
- Suspension from AIUB for one or more academic terms.
- Permanent expulsion from the university.

The severity of the disciplinary action depends on the applicable
disciplinary rules and circumstances of the violation.


KEYWORDS:
disciplinary suspension, academic misconduct,
exam cheating, plagiarism, code of conduct,
FD, suspension, expulsion


================================================================================
TOPIC 12: DEAN'S LIST
================================================================================

The Dean's List recognizes strong academic performance during a regular
semester.

According to the stated policy, eligibility requires:

- Term GPA between 3.75 and 4.00.
- Completion of a full-time credit load, stated as a minimum of
  approximately 12–15 credits.
- No F grade during the semester.
- No UW grade during the semester.
- No I grade during the semester.
- No W grade during the semester.
- No disciplinary action history.


KEYWORDS:
Dean's List, Dean List, term GPA 3.75,
term GPA 4.00, full-time credits, 12 credits,
15 credits, F, UW, I, W, academic honors


================================================================================
TOPIC 13: HONORABLE MENTION
================================================================================

Honorable Mention recognizes students with strong semester academic
performance below the Dean's List range.

According to the stated policy, eligibility requires:

- Term GPA between 3.50 and 3.74.
- Full credit load.
- Clean academic record.


KEYWORDS:
Honorable Mention, honorable mention GPA,
3.50 GPA, 3.74 GPA, academic recognition,
semester honors

TOPIC 14: GRADUATION HONORS / CONVOCATION AWARDS
================================================================================

AIUB graduation honors are based on the student's final CGPA.

Summa Cum Laude:
- Final CGPA: 3.90–4.00
- Recognition: Highest Distinction

Magna Cum Laude:
- Final CGPA: 3.80–3.89
- Recognition: High Distinction

Cum Laude:
- Final CGPA: 3.70–3.79
- Recognition: Distinction


KEYWORDS:
graduation honors, convocation honors,
Summa Cum Laude, Magna Cum Laude, Cum Laude,
highest distinction, high distinction, distinction,
final CGPA


================================================================================
TOPIC 15: ELIGIBILITY RESTRICTIONS FOR GRADUATION HONORS
================================================================================

The stated policy includes additional restrictions for graduation honors.

A student seeking graduation honors must:

- Complete the degree within the minimum prescribed duration.
- Have no F grades on the official transcript.
- Have no UW grades on the official transcript.
- Have no W grades on the official transcript.
- Have no retaken courses on the official transcript.
- Maintain a clean proctorial record.
- Maintain a clean disciplinary record.


KEYWORDS:
graduation honors eligibility, honors restrictions,
F grade honors, UW honors, W grade honors,
retake honors, transcript, proctorial record,
disciplinary record


================================================================================
TOPIC 16: RETAKING F COURSES AND CGPA
================================================================================

For undergraduate students, the stated policy provides special treatment
for retaken F courses.

First four retaken F courses:
- The new grade replaces the previous F in CGPA calculation.

From the fifth retaken F course onward:
- Both the previous F and the new grade are factored into CGPA calculation.


KEYWORDS:
retake F, F retake, first four retakes,
fifth retake, CGPA replacement,
grade replacement, undergraduate retake

Q: Does W affect CGPA?

A:
According to the stated policy, a W grade does not affect CGPA.


Q: Does I affect CGPA?

A:
According to the stated policy, an I grade does not affect CGPA while it

Q: Does F affect CGPA?

A:
Yes. An F carries 0.00 Grade Point and is factored into CGPA according to

Q: What CGPA is required for Good Academic Standing?

A:
A CGPA of 2.50 or above is required for Good Academic Standing.


Q: What happens if my CGPA is between 2.00 and 2.49?

A:
The stated policy places students in this range under Academic Warning
depending on whether the semester GPA or overall CGPA falls within the
specified range.


Q: What happens if my CGPA falls below 2.00?

A:
The student may be placed on Academic Probation. Probation may involve
a reduced course load, mandatory advising, and other restrictions.


Q: How many credits can I take during Academic Probation?

A:
The stated probationary credit limit is approximately 9–12 credits per
term.


Q: What happens if I remain on probation for two semesters?

A:
A student who remains on Academic Probation for two consecutive semesters
without raising the CGPA above 2.00 may be subject to Academic Dismissal.


Q: What GPA is required for the Dean's List?

A:
The stated requirement is a Term GPA of 3.75–4.00 with the required
full-time course load and a clean academic record.


Q: Can I get Dean's List if I have a W grade?

A:
According to the stated policy, a W grade during the semester makes the
student ineligible for Dean's List recognition for that term.


Q: Can I get Dean's List if I have a UW grade?

A:
According to the stated policy, a UW grade during the semester makes the
student ineligible for Dean's List recognition for that term.


Q: Can I get Dean's List if I have an F grade?

A:
According to the stated policy, an F grade during the semester makes the
student ineligible for Dean's List recognition for that term.


Q: What GPA is required for Honorable Mention?

A:
The stated requirement is a Term GPA of 3.50–3.74 with the required
full credit load and a clean academic record.


Q: What CGPA is required for Summa Cum Laude?

A:
The stated final CGPA range for Summa Cum Laude is 3.90–4.00.


Q: What CGPA is required for Magna Cum Laude?

A:
The stated final CGPA range for Magna Cum Laude is 3.80–3.89.


Q: What CGPA is required for Cum Laude?

A:
The stated final CGPA range for Cum Laude is 3.70–3.79.


Q: Can I receive graduation honors if I retook a course?

A:
According to the stated policy, students with retaken courses on the
official transcript are not eligible for graduation honors.


Q: Can I receive graduation honors if I have a W grade?

A:

Q: Can I receive graduation honors if I have a UW grade?

A:
According to the stated policy, a UW grade on the official transcript
makes the student ineligible for graduation honors.
