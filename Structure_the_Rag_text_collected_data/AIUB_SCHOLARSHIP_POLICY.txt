AIUB SCHOLARSHIP POLICY

University: American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB)

Policy Purpose:
The purpose of the scholarship policy is to help deserving AIUB students bridge the gap between education costs and their financial resources.

Important:
Scholarships mentioned in this file provide tuition fee waiver only.

==================================================
SCHOLARSHIP CATEGORIES
==================================================

Academic Scholarships:
1. Merit Based Scholarship for Freshman Students
2. Academic Scholarship for Regular Students
3. Dr. Anwarul Abedin Scholarship for Female Undergraduate Students
4. Mrs. Hasna Abedin Scholarship

Extra-Curricular Scholarships:
1. National Talent Scholarship
2. Sports Scholarship
3. Club Scholarship
4. Scholarship for Students with Disability
5. Scholarship for Students from Underrepresented Groups


==================================================
GENERAL SCHOLARSHIP RULE
==================================================

A student is entitled to receive a scholarship in only one category.

If a student qualifies for scholarships in more than one category, the student will receive the scholarship with the higher percentage, where applicable.


==================================================
MERIT BASED SCHOLARSHIP FOR FRESHMAN STUDENTS
==================================================

Eligible Students:
Freshman students.

Eligibility Criteria:
1. GPA 5.00 (Golden) OR GPA 5.00 in both SSC and HSC.
2. O-Level students must have 5 A grades at a time.
3. A-Level students must have 2 A grades at a time.
4. No grade lower than B for O-Level and A-Level.

Application Procedure:
1. Eligible applicants must sit for a Scholarship Exam.
2. Applicants must pass the Scholarship Exam with a satisfactory grade.
3. The acceptable passing grade may vary from time to time.
4. The Scholarship Committee decides the acceptable passing grade.
5. The number of recipients may vary depending on available slots.
6. Scholarship award depends on the availability of slots.


==================================================
ACADEMIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR REGULAR STUDENTS
==================================================

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Must complete two regular semesters (Spring and Fall).
2. Can apply only in a regular semester.
3. Undergraduate students must take 14-15 credits per regular semester.
4. Graduate students must take 12 credits per regular semester.
5. EMBA students must take 4 courses per regular semester.
6. Must have CGPA 3.75 or above.
7. No grade lower than B+.
8. No F (Fail).
9. No I (Incomplete).
10. No W (Withdrawn).
11. No UW (Unofficial Withdrawn).
12. Must not drop any course.
13. Must not retake any course.
14. Must have no disciplinary action.

Important:
The number of recipients may vary depending on available slots.


==================================================
DR. ANWARUL ABEDIN SCHOLARSHIP
==================================================

Eligible Students:
Female undergraduate students.

Tuition Fee Coverage:
100% tuition fee waiver.

Frequency:
Every regular semester, Fall and Spring.

Slots:
One student per faculty, based on available slots declared by the Scholarship Committee.

Eligibility Criteria:
1. CGPA must be 3.8 or above.
2. No grade below B+.
3. Must be a regular full-time/full-credit student.
4. Must take 12-15 credits.
5. Must have no semester gap.
6. Must provide Certification of No Disciplinary Action from OSA.
7. Must provide Certification of Good Moral Character from OSA.
8. Must be a member of an AIUB club.
9. Must actively participate in department/course extracurricular activities.
10. Must not receive any other scholarship or financial assistance from AIUB or another organization.

Renewal:
The grant is renewed every regular semester subject to maintaining the CGPA and other criteria.

==================================================
NATIONAL TALENT SCHOLARSHIP
==================================================

Eligibility:
Students with achievements at the national level recognized by the relevant national authority.

Selection Process:
1. Student must submit an application.
2. Student must provide supporting documents.
3. Scholarship Committee evaluates eligibility.
4. Scholarship Committee recommends the tuition fee waiver percentage.

MOU:
National talent scholarships under an MOU between AIUB and national-level organizations follow the terms of the MOU.

Important:
The Scholarship Committee selects recipients and has complete discretion to extend scholarship to an individual student.


==================================================
SPORTS SCHOLARSHIP
==================================================

Eligible Students:
Selected students who participate in sports activities for AIUB.

Eligibility:
1. Must participate in sports activities for the university.
2. Must demonstrate financial need.

Waiver Percentage:
The Scholarship Committee decides the percentage based on the applicant's contribution in sports.


==================================================
CLUB SCHOLARSHIP
==================================================

Eligible Students:
Selected students who are members of AIUB student clubs.

Eligibility:
1. Must be a regular club member for at least one year.
2. Must contribute to the achievements of the club.
3. Must demonstrate financial need.

Waiver Percentage:
The Scholarship Committee decides the percentage based on the applicant's contribution to club activities.


==================================================
SCHOLARSHIP FOR STUDENTS WITH DISABILITY
==================================================

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Must be a full-time AIUB student.
2. Must have a disability as defined by the Bangladesh Persons with Disabilities Rights and Protection Act 2013.
3. Must provide documentation verifying the disability.
4. Must meet minimum standards for credits, semester, GPA, CGPA, and continuity of studies.
5. Must submit an application to the Registrar's Office within the semester deadline.
6. Scholarship percentage depends on the Scholarship Committee's recommendation.
7. Assessment may include application review and/or interview.

Final Decision:
The final decision is at the discretion of AIUB Management.


==================================================
SCHOLARSHIP FOR STUDENTS FROM UNDERREPRESENTED GROUPS
==================================================

Underrepresented Groups May Include:
1. Ethnic minorities.
2. Low-income students.
3. Non-traditional students.
4. 3rd Gender.
5. Newly settled refugees.

Eligibility Criteria:
1. Must be a full-time AIUB student.
2. Must provide documentation verifying underrepresented group status.
3. Must meet minimum standards for credits, semester, GPA, CGPA, and continuity of studies.
4. Must submit an application to the Registrar's Office within the semester deadline.
5. Scholarship percentage depends on the Scholarship Committee's recommendation.
6. Assessment may include application review and/or interview.

Final Decision:
The final decision is at the discretion of AIUB Management.

Non-Traditional Student Definition:
A non-traditional student is an applicant who has completed a degree equivalent to the requirement but the degree is not specifically mentioned in the UGC list of requirements.

A degree exclusively barred by the UGC list is not acceptable.

Degree equivalency must be recommended by the admission interview committee, faculty dean, or administration based on document verification.

Final admission decision is at the discretion of AIUB Management.


==================================================
SCHOLARSHIP RENEWAL
==================================================

Renewal Frequency:
Every regular semester.

Renewal Requirements:
1. Undergraduate students must take 14-15 credits per regular semester.
2. Graduate students must take 12 credits per regular semester.
3. Must be enrolled in all academic courses during preceding and subsequent semesters.
4. Must have no disciplinary record.
5. Must maintain the required CGPA for the respective scholarship.
6. Must have no F (Fail).
7. Must have no I (Incomplete).
8. Must have no W (Withdrawn).
9. Must have no UW (Unofficial Withdrawn).
10. Must not drop courses.
11. Must not retake courses.
12. Must maintain attendance above 80%.

Important:
If a student is eligible for more than one scholarship or discount, only one will apply. The higher scholarship or discount will be applicable.
